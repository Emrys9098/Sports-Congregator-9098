Opinions on sports stuff - NFL Draft Edition

First off, this is kind of late for the draft but I just wanted to make some comments and give opinions about certain picks that I have 2 cents for

Here goes

Raiders

I like the Mendoza pick, while I do not see them being a dominant force just yet adding Mendoza plus signing Kirk Cousins gives them a good QB room for now and later plus the offseason additions gives me confidence they'll end up being a good team later on as long as ownership or coaching doesn't get too hasty

I predict the Raiders to end between 6-8 wins with clear signs of something to come near the end of the season

Cardinals

I really don't know what to say about them honestly. The team has so many holes and they pick Jeremiyah Love at #3 overall. Only reason I could see is them embracing the tank and/or expecting their current guys to take a step up.

I predict the Cardinals to be part of the race to the bottom, I believe in the theory that anything can happen, they just don't seem to have anything going for them. 5-12 at best

Rams

Ty Simpson. I understand that it's time to get the succession plan set up but they have the reigning MVP and a wide open Super Bowl window and while I kinda expected it at the same time it could very well be the downfall of the 2026-2027 Rams to have not taken a WR early. Les Snead is on the list of GMs that I assume has a method to the madness so who knows but the pick confuses me.

I predict the Rams to end between 11-14 wins and make another deep, potentially Super Bowl run especially with the secondary upgrades and rumoured to be doing a Monty and Gibbs with Kyren Williams and Blake Corum

Cowboys

After they traded Micah Parsons I really thought their defense was dead in the water since they traded a player who singlehandedly elevates a defense. Picking Caleb Downs and Malachi Lawrence I believe their defense will be better than the JV squad they ran out last season. I don't see them being a worldbeater but if their defense is just competent next season I could see them fighting for the NFC East crown with how potent the offense is.

I predict the Cowboys will end between 9-12 wins. They won't be a contender but as long as the defense isn't nearly as bad they'll be fine, likely keep up with a Cowboys tradition and be a divisional out at best

Browns

Last take for this edition. Browns had a stellar 2025 draft class with guys like Schwesinger, Graham, Fannin Jr., and Judkins. If the Browns have as good of a 2026 draft as they did 2025 they could very well be a contender. It is the Browns so I know to taper my expectations but I could see them being at the very least a thorn in teams sides. While I do believe in Shedeur Sanders I will say that outside of WR the biggest thing holding the Browns back was QB play. With the Browns getting WR during the draft and upgrades to the O-line I could see them being a very good team as long as QB play steps up.

This one might be a hot take but I see the Browns getting between 7-10 wins and at the very least being one of the teams that is in the running for the 7 seed during the late slate in week 18 at 9-8 or 10-7. QB play will likely hold them back but I see the overall team being much better than they were in 2025.
